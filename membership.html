Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you join.

Is membership really free to join?

Yes — Community Membership is completely free and gets you BFF app access, monthly event invitations, and our community updates.

What do I get with Elite Membership?

Everything in Community Membership, plus exclusive local business discounts, access to monthly wellness experiences, and business resources.

How do I access the BFF app?

Once you join, you'll get an invite to our private community space for message boards, event RSVPs, and getting to know other members.

Do I have to attend every event?

Not at all. Come to what excites you, whenever it works for you — there's no minimum attendance to stay a member.

How does the Founders Council work?

It's a standing group of our most loyal members who help shape BYFY's future. Applications are open on our Get Involved page.

Where are events held?

Mostly around Jacksonville, FL. Check the Events page for the full calendar and locations.
